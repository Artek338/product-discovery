## QUICK REFERENCE CARD

### 🎯 Business Analyst Checklist

**Before Starting Analysis:**

- [ ] Understand the product idea clearly (no vague descriptions)
- [ ] Identify target customer (specific persona, not "everyone")
- [ ] Confirm what problem this solves
- [ ] Set expectations: this is GO/NO-GO, not cheerleading

**During Market Research:**

- [ ] Find TAM/SAM/SOM with sources (not assumptions)
- [ ] Identify 3-5 direct competitors with pricing
- [ ] Check market growth rate (YoY %)
- [ ] Validate demand (are people paying for solutions now?)
- [ ] Document every claim with URL + date

**During Risk Assessment:**

- [ ] Identify 3-5 critical risks
- [ ] For each: probability + impact + mitigation
- [ ] Don't hide elephants (uncomfortable truths)
- [ ] Pre-Mortem: imagine project failed - why?

**Before Delivering Verdict:**

- [ ] Score is calculated (not gut feeling)
- [ ] Werdykt is clear: GO/NO-GO/OSTROŻNE-GO
- [ ] Every number has source cited
- [ ] Risks are named with mitigation plans
- [ ] Next steps are concrete (not "do more research")
- [ ] User knows EXACTLY what to do next

**JTBD Analysis:**

- [ ] Conducted "switch interviews" (people who recently switched)
- [ ] Mapped all 4 forces (Push, Pull, Anxiety, Habit)
- [ ] Have real customer quotes for each force
- [ ] Product strategy addresses each force
- [ ] Identified trigger event (why NOW?)

**Discovery Habits:**

- [ ] Planned 2-3 customer interviews this week
- [ ] Story-based questions (not hypothetical)
- [ ] Recorded and synthesized as team
- [ ] Updated Opportunity Solution Tree
- [ ] Designed experiments < 2 weeks

**Red Flags to Challenge:**

- [ ] "Market is huge" → What's realistic SOM Year 1?
- [ ] "No competition" → Did you search? Or no demand?
- [ ] "We'll charge premium" → Why will they pay more?
- [ ] "Viral growth" → What's the actual loop?
- [ ] "Easy to build" → What's the hard part?
