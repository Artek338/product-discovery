# Continuous Discovery Habits

**Author:** Teresa Torres  
**Source:** "Continuous Discovery Habits" + industry leaders #67

---

## Problem

Discovery happens once at the beginning, then we build for months without talking to customers.

## Solution

Weekly touchpoints with customers.

---

## The Product Trio

- Product Manager
- Designer
- Tech Lead

**Work together, talk to customers together.**

---

## Weekly Cadence

```
Monday: Plan week's interviews (who, what to learn)
Tue-Thu: Conduct 2-3 customer interviews (30 min each)
Friday: Synthesis session - update Opportunity Solution Tree
```

---

## Interview Structure (30 min)

```
0-5 min: Rapport building
5-25 min: Story-based questions
  - "Tell me about the last time you [did X]"
  - "Walk me through your process"
  - "What was frustrating about that?"
25-30 min: Prototype feedback (if applicable)
```

---

## Key Principles

### 1. Story-based questions (not hypothetical)

- ❌ "Would you use a feature that..."
- ✅ "Tell me about the last time you tried to..."

### 2. Specific instances (not general opinions)

- ❌ "Do you generally find X frustrating?"
- ✅ "What happened the last time you used X?"

### 3. Automated recruiting

- In-app prompt: "Help us improve [feature]"
- Calendly link
- $25 Amazon gift card

### 4. Synthesis as a team

- Watch recordings together
- Update Opportunity Solution Tree
- Decide next experiments

## Interview Snapshot Template

```markdown
## Interview: [Name] - [Date]

**Quick Facts:**
- Role: [Job title]
- Company: [Size, industry]
- Use case: [How they use product]

**Key Quotes:**
- "[Exact quote about pain point]"
- "[Exact quote about desired outcome]"

**Opportunities Discovered:**
- [Pain point 1]
- [Pain point 2]

**Assumptions Validated/Invalidated:**
- ✅ Validated: [Assumption]
- ❌ Invalidated: [Assumption]

**Next Steps:**
- [Follow-up question for next interview]
- [Experiment to run]
```

---

## Assumption Testing

Przed budową, wypisz assumptions:

```markdown
## Assumptions for [Feature]

**Value Assumptions** (czy to rozwiązuje problem?)
1. Users struggle with [X] - Test: Interview 5 users
2. Current solution is [Y] - Test: Observe workflow

**Usability Assumptions** (czy potrafią użyć?)
1. Users understand [concept] - Test: Prototype + think-aloud
2. Users can complete [task] in <2 min - Test: Usability test

**Feasibility Assumptions** (czy możemy zbudować?)
1. API supports [feature] - Test: Tech spike (2 days)
2. Performance is acceptable - Test: Load test

**Viability Assumptions** (czy to biznesowo ma sens?)
1. Users will pay $X - Test: Fake door test
2. CAC < $Y - Test: Landing page + ads
```

### Prioritize Assumptions

- Highest risk first (biggest unknown)
- Cheapest to test first (if equal risk)
