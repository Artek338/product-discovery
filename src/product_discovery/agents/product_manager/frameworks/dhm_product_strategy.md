# Product Strategy - DHM Model

**Author:** Gibson Biddle  
**Source:** PM industry leaders, ex-VP Product at Netflix

---

## Problem

Product teams struggle to create coherent strategies that balance customer delight with business viability and competitive advantage.

## The DHM Model

**DHM** = Delight customers in **H**ard-to-copy, **M**argin-enhancing ways

### The Three Questions

#### 1. Delight (D) - How will you delight customers?

**Goal:** Create products that solve real problems and create emotional connections

**For startups:** Build something 10x better than alternatives to drive word-of-mouth growth

**Key questions:**

- What customer needs/pain points are we solving?
- How do we create an emotional connection?
- What makes this delightful, not just functional?

#### 2. Hard-to-Copy (H) - What makes it defensible?

**Goal:** Develop unique advantages competitors cannot easily replicate

**Examples:**

- Strong brand identity
- Proprietary technology
- Intellectual property
- Network effects
- High switching costs
- Unique data/insights

**Key questions:**

- What can we build that competitors can't copy?
- What moats protect our position?
- How do we create lasting competitive advantage?

#### 3. Margin-Enhancing (M) - How does it make money?

**Goal:** Generate incremental revenue and margin for continued investment

**Key questions:**

- How does this generate revenue?
- What's the business model?
- Can we afford to keep investing in delight?
- How do we balance customer value with profitability?

## How to Use

### 1. Strategy as Hypothesis

Treat your product strategy as a hypothesis to test:

```
We believe that [DELIGHT]
Will create [HARD-TO-COPY advantage]
And generate [MARGIN]
```

### 2. Netflix Example (Gibson Biddle)

**Delight:** Personalized recommendations + binge-watching experience  
**Hard-to-Copy:** Proprietary recommendation algorithm + exclusive content  
**Margin:** Subscription model with low marginal cost per user

### 3. Evaluation Framework

For each product decision, ask:

- ✅ Does this delight customers?
- ✅ Is it hard for competitors to copy?
- ✅ Does it enhance our margins?

If answer is "no" to any, reconsider the decision.

## Key Insights

> "Product strategy is about making choices on how to delight customers in hard-to-copy, margin-enhancing ways." - Gibson Biddle

**Common Mistakes:**

- ❌ Focusing only on delight without defensibility
- ❌ Building features competitors can easily copy
- ❌ Ignoring business model and margins
- ❌ Treating strategy as static, not hypothesis

**Success Factors:**

- ✅ Balance all three elements (D, H, M)
- ✅ Test and iterate on strategy
- ✅ Use strategy to guide product decisions
- ✅ Communicate strategy clearly to team
