# Four Fits Growth Framework

**Author:** Brian Balfour  
**Source:** Reforge, ex-VP Growth at HubSpot

---

## Problem

Companies focus only on Product-Market Fit, but sustainable growth requires four interconnected "fits" working together.

## The Four Fits

For companies to achieve $100M+ valuation and sustained growth, they need:

### 1. Market-Product Fit

**Question:** Does your product solve a real problem for a specific market?

**Key Elements:**

- Target market definition
- Problem-solution fit
- Value proposition
- Product differentiation

### 2. Product-Channel Fit ⭐

**Question:** Does your product fit the distribution channel?

**Critical Insight:** Products are built to fit channels, not the other way around

**Why it matters:**

- Companies don't control channel rules (e.g., social algorithms, SEO)
- Product must be "molded" to channel constraints
- Successful companies derive significant growth from ONE well-aligned channel

**Examples:**

- **Pinterest:** Product designed for SEO (rich pins, descriptions)
- **Instagram:** Product designed for mobile-first sharing
- **Dropbox:** Product designed for viral referrals (free storage for invites)

**Channel Types:**

- Paid ads (Facebook, Google)
- SEO/Content
- Virality/Referrals
- Sales
- Partnerships

### 3. Channel-Model Fit

**Question:** Does your business model support your channel economics?

**Key Metrics:**

- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- LTV (Lifetime Value)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target 3:1+)
- Payback period

**Example:**

- High-touch sales channel requires high LTV products
- Viral channel works with low/no CAC products

### 4. Model-Market Fit

**Question:** Does your business model fit the market size and dynamics?

**Key Elements:**

- Market size (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- Pricing strategy
- Unit economics
- Scalability

---

## How to Use

### 1. Assess All Four Fits

Don't optimize one fit in isolation - they're interconnected:

```
Market ←→ Product ←→ Channel ←→ Model
```

### 2. Product-Channel Fit Deep Dive

**Step 1: Identify potential channels**

- Where does your target market spend time?
- What channels do competitors use?

**Step 2: Design product for channel**

- What product attributes fit the channel?
- How can you optimize for channel algorithms?
- What features enable channel growth?

**Step 3: Test and iterate**

- Run small experiments
- Measure channel-specific metrics
- Double down on what works

### 3. Dynamic Fits

**Important:** Fits can break over time

- New channels emerge (TikTok, AI)
- Old channels change rules (Facebook algorithm)
- Market dynamics shift
- Requires continuous re-evaluation

## Growth Model Framework

### Qualitative Model

**Components:**

1. **Inputs:** What drives growth? (users, content, transactions)
2. **Outputs:** What are the results? (revenue, engagement)
3. **Loops:** What creates compounding growth?

### Quantitative Model

**Key Drivers:**

- Acquisition rate
- Activation rate
- Retention rate
- Revenue per user
- Referral rate

**Formula:**

```
Growth = (Acquisition × Activation × Retention) + Referrals
```

## Key Insights

> "Products are built to fit channels. Companies that achieve $100M+ valuation have all four fits working together." - Brian Balfour

**Common Mistakes:**

- ❌ Focusing only on Product-Market Fit
- ❌ Expecting channels to adapt to product
- ❌ Optimizing one fit in isolation
- ❌ Ignoring channel changes over time

**Success Factors:**

- ✅ Assess all four fits holistically
- ✅ Design product for channel from day one
- ✅ Monitor fit health continuously
- ✅ Re-evaluate as market/channels evolve
