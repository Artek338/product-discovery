# STAKEHOLDER MAP: [Project Name]

> **Purpose:** Identify who holds the power, who uses the tool, and who will try to kill it.
> **When to use:** Required for B2B projects. Optional (but recommended) for B2C — focus on End User and Influencer.

## 1. The Cast of Characters

| Role | Who is it? (Job Title/Persona) | Power Level (1-10) | Interest Level (1-10) |
|------|--------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| **Decision Maker**<br>(The Budget Holder) | [e.g. CEO, CFO] | 10 | 8 |
| **End User**<br>(The daily operator) | [e.g. Marketing Intern] | 2 | 10 |
| **Project Champion**<br>(Your internal ally) | [e.g. CMO] | 7 | 9 |
| **Blocker / Saboteur**<br>(Threatened by change) | [e.g. IT Manager, Agency] | 8 | 5 |
| **Influencer**<br>(Trusted advisor) | [e.g. Consultant, Blogger] | 5 | 3 |

> [!NOTE]
> **B2C/Solo projects:** The "Decision Maker" and "End User" are often the same person.
> Skip the Blocker/Champion analysis and focus on understanding the End User's habits and fears.

## 2. Psychological Analysis

### 👑 The Decision Maker

- **Win (Motivation):** [What makes them look good? e.g. "Saving 20% budget"]
- **Fear (Risk):** [What keeps them up at night? e.g. "Implementation takes 6 months"]
- **Trigger:** [What event causes them to buy? e.g. "Quarterly Review in Q4"]

### 👷 The End User

- **Win (Motivation):** [e.g. "Getting home at 5 PM instead of 7 PM"]
- **Fear (Risk):** [e.g. "The AI will replace my job"]
- **Current Habit:** [What do they do now? How entrenched is this behavior?]

### 🛑 The Blocker

- **Why do they resist?** [e.g. "They lose control of data", "Their job becomes redundant"]
- **How to neutralize?** [e.g. "Give them admin rights", "Make them co-owner of rollout"]

## 3. Communication Strategy

| Stakeholder | Talk to When? | Key Message | Communication Channel |
|-------------|--------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Champion | First | "Here's how this makes your team win" | 1:1 meeting |
| End User | Second | "This saves you X hours/week" | Demo / Prototype |
| Decision Maker | Third (with Champion) | "ROI: $X saved, Y% efficiency" | Executive summary |
| Blocker | Last (carefully) | "You keep control of Z" | Include in planning |

## 4. Action Items

- **Who do we talk to FIRST?** [Usually the Champion or User]
- **Who signs the check?** [Decision Maker]
- **Who kills the deal?** [Blocker — and what's our plan?]
- **What commitment do we ask for?** [See `knowledge-base/evidence_levels.md` Level 3+]
