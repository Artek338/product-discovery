# Evidence Levels: How to Grade Your Data

> **Purpose:** Not all "feedback" is equal. Use this scale to grade the evidence supporting your assumptions.
> **Rule:** Decisions must be based on Level 2+ evidence.

## 🔴 Level 0: Pure Opinion (Discard)

* "I think this is a good idea."
* "I would use this."
* Compliments ("Cool app!").
* **Verdict:** Worthless. Do not build.

## 🟡 Level 1: Weak Evidence (Observe)

* "I have this problem" (stated belief).
* Hypothetical future behavior ("I will buy it when...").
* Survey results without context.
* **Verdict:** Interesting, but not proof. Dig deeper.

## 🟢 Level 2: Strong Evidence (Validate)

* **Past Behavior:** "Last week I spent 3 hours fixing this."
* **Workaround:** "I currently use Excel + Zapier to solve this."
* **Active Search:** User is actively looking for a solution *now*.
* **Verdict:** Validated Problem. Safe to proceed to Solution Design.

## 🔵 Level 3: Commitment (Build)

* **Time Investment:** User agrees to a 60-min deep dive call.
* **Reputation Risk:** User introduces you to their boss/peers.
* **Data Access:** User gives you their real data/login to test.
* **Verdict:** Validated Solution interest.

## 🟣 Level 4: Skin in the Game (Scale)

* **LOI:** Letter of Intent signed.
* **Waitlist:** Email + Phone number verified.
* **Pre-order:** Nominal amount paid ($1).
* **Verdict:** Validated Market.

## 🏆 Level 5: Cash (Success)

* **Pre-payment:** Full price paid before product exists.
* **Contract:** Signed multi-year deal.
* **Verdict:** Product-Market Fit.

---

## Enforcement Rules

| Phase | Minimum Evidence | What it means |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Phase 2 (Research)** | **Level 2+** for the Problem | User described past behavior or active workaround |
| **Phase 3 (Validation)** | **Level 2+** with at least one **Level 3** signal | User committed time, reputation, or data access |
| **Go/No-Go** | Multiple **Level 2+**, ideally one **Level 3+** | Enough validated facts to justify building |

> [!NOTE]
> For solo/startup projects, Level 3 (time commitment, data access) is realistic.
> Level 4-5 (LOI, pre-payment) applies mainly to B2B/enterprise sales cycles.
